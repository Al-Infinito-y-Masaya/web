# Mangroove

## Platform

web

## Purpose and evidence

Mangroove lets people inspect NISAR observations of mangroves, their relation to modeled tide and the limits of the analysis. The existing application and its contract remain available. The user authorized a separate experimental route for the independently processed estuaries, beginning with Aserradores.

## Experimental extension

Read-only map, acquisition selection, cell inspection, time series and scientific quality. Use the actual contract 0.2 products, never sample data. Preserve null values, distinguish internal controls from independent validation, and show incomplete tidal coverage. Publication identity is distinct from contract version. The audience and long-term product roadmap beyond this inspection workflow remain open.

## Existing identity

The implemented Mangroove identity is authoritative: paper, forest green, ocean ink, Jost/Josefin Sans, and data colors defined by the source. This extension does not redesign the existing application.
